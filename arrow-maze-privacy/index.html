Arrow Maze: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026 · App: Arrow Maze: Switch Puzzle (package com.ahsnaz.outflip)

Arrow Maze is a puzzle game made by an independent developer. This policy explains what happens to information when you play.

Summary

  • Your game progress and settings stay on your phone. There are no accounts and no sign-in.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob (Google) collects some data to show and measure ads, described below.
  • An optional Remove ads purchase is handled entirely by Google Play.

What stays on your phone

  • Your game progress (levels, stars, hints, daily streak) and settings are saved only on your device.
  • If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled on your device by Android. No server sends it.
  • You can erase everything at any time in Settings → Reset progress, or by uninstalling the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Arrow Maze uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may automatically collect and process:

  • your device's IP address (which can indicate an approximate location),
  • device and advertising identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID),
  • ad interactions (for example, whether an ad was viewed or tapped),
  • diagnostic information (for example, app launch time and performance).

Google uses this data to show ads, to measure them, for analytics and to prevent fraud. It is encrypted in transit. In regions where consent is required (such as the EEA and the UK), Arrow Maze asks for your choice with Google's consent message before ads are requested, and you can change it later in Settings → Privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. How Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites · Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Rewarded videos are optional. You only see one when you choose "Watch a video" to get extra hints.

Purchases (Google Play)

The optional Remove ads purchase is processed by Google Play. Arrow Maze never receives your payment details. It only learns whether the purchase is active, so it can turn ads off and restore the purchase on a new phone.

Other Google Play features

  • When you choose Rate now, Google Play may show its own rating dialog. Your rating goes to Google Play; Arrow Maze never sees it.
  • Arrow Maze asks Google Play whether a newer version is available, so it can offer an update.

What we, the developer, collect

Nothing directly. Arrow Maze has no server of its own, no analytics SDK and no accounts. We do not sell personal information.

Children

Arrow Maze is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
