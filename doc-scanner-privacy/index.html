1. Overview

Doc Scanner is a mobile application that allows users to scan documents, import images and PDFs, recognize text, organize documents, and share, print, or download files.

Doc Scanner stores documents in the app's private storage on your device. The developer does not operate a server for this app and does not receive your documents or recognized text through a developer-operated service.

Google ML Kit components used by the app may transmit technical diagnostics and identifiers to Google, as described in Section 4. Those components are operated by Google, and this policy describes Google's published statements about them.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Doc Scanner stores the following information locally, as applicable:

  • Document titles and creation dates.
  • Scanned page images.
  • Generated PDF files.
  • Imported PDF files.
  • Text recognized from scanned documents.
  • File paths and other document-management information.

This information is stored in the app's private storage and in a local database on your device.

Temporary copies may be created during scanning, importing, or sharing. The app is designed to remove temporary copies after the relevant operation. Google's document-scanning component may also create temporary files in the app's cache.

Importing images and PDFs. When you import images, Doc Scanner uses Android's photo-selection interface and copies the images you select into its storage. When you open a PDF from another application, Doc Scanner copies the PDF into its private storage. Duplicate detection is performed on the device.

Doc Scanner does not require an account or sign-in.

3. Camera and Document Scanning

Doc Scanner does not request the Android camera permission. When you start a scan, Google's document-scanning component uses the device camera through Google Play services. If Google's scanner is unavailable on your device, Doc Scanner may fall back to your device's camera app to capture a picture.

Google's ML Kit Terms state that ML Kit processes input data, such as images, on the device and does not send that input data or its outputs to Google's servers. The Document Scanner is delivered through Google Play services, which downloads its models, scanning logic and user interface, so its availability may depend on Google Play services and on the download of required components.

4. Google ML Kit and Technical Diagnostics

Doc Scanner uses Google ML Kit for text recognition (bundled with the app) and Google's document-scanning component through Google Play services.

Google's ML Kit Terms state that input data (such as images, video or text) is processed on the device and that ML Kit does not send that input data or its outputs to Google's servers. ML Kit may contact Google's servers for purposes such as bug fixes, updated models and hardware-accelerator compatibility information.

Google also documents the transmission of technical diagnostics and usage metrics to Google over HTTPS. These may include:

  • Device and application information.
  • Application performance metrics.
  • API configuration and feature information.
  • Input and output sizes.
  • Event types and error information.
  • Identifiers used for diagnostics and usage analytics.

Google states that these metrics support purposes such as measuring performance, debugging, maintaining and improving ML Kit APIs, and detecting misuse or abuse.

Google's ML Kit data disclosure describes identifiers in general terms: per-installation identifiers that Google says are not intended to uniquely identify a user or physical device and, for some features, a device identifier. Google's disclosure does not list which identifiers apply to each individual ML Kit feature, so this policy does not state which identifiers apply to text recognition or to the document scanner.

This diagnostic information may be transmitted to Google.

Doc Scanner does not provide its own in-app setting to control this diagnostic transmission. The Google ML Kit documentation we reviewed does not describe a way for developers or users to turn it off.

For further information, consult:

  • ML Kit data disclosure: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure
  • ML Kit Terms and Privacy: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The Google ML Kit documentation we reviewed does not state how long this diagnostic information is retained.

5. Internet Permissions and Third-Party Services

The app includes the Android INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permissions through its Google-related dependencies.

Google ML Kit and Google Play services may perform network operations for diagnostic reporting and to obtain required components or updates. These services are operated by Google, not by the developer.

Doc Scanner does not currently include its own advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, account, or cloud-backup integration.

6. Sharing, Printing and Downloading

Doc Scanner allows users to copy recognized text, share text or files, print PDFs, and download PDF copies.

When you choose to share or print content, Android may present another application or destination to complete the action. The receiving application or service handles the information according to its own privacy practices.

Files downloaded to your device's public Downloads location are separate from the copies stored in Doc Scanner. Deleting a document from Doc Scanner does not delete previously downloaded, shared, or printed copies.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Documents remain in the app's private storage until you delete them, clear the app's data, or uninstall the application.

Deleting a document permanently removes its database record and associated files from the app's storage. This action cannot be undone.

Copies previously downloaded, shared, or printed must be managed separately.

Uninstalling Doc Scanner or clearing the app's data removes the documents stored in the app's private storage. The current version does not provide cloud backup, export, or a document-restoration service.

Doc Scanner does not provide a separate in-app mechanism to request deletion of Google-held ML Kit diagnostic information. For information about data handled by Google, consult Google's privacy documentation (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

8. Security

Doc Scanner stores documents in the app's private storage, protected by Android's application sandbox.

The app does not add its own encryption-at-rest mechanism or an app-lock or biometric-lock feature. Anyone who can unlock your device and access the app may be able to view documents stored in it.

Files downloaded or shared outside the app are subject to the security protections of the destination and device.

Please avoid storing highly sensitive documents unless you understand these limitations.

9. Children's Privacy

Doc Scanner is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. Google ML Kit components used by the app may transmit technical diagnostics and identifiers to Google, as described in this Privacy Policy. The developer does not operate a server that receives scanned documents or recognized text from the app. If you are under 13, please do not use Doc Scanner. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app, please contact us at contact.ahsnaz@gmail.com.
